Freedium (formerly Medium Unlocker) lets you read articles from seven leading publishers without a subscription, by routing them through the freedium.cfd mirrors and archive services.

Paste an article URL or share it directly from another app — Freedium opens it instantly through your chosen mirror.

SUPPORTED PUBLISHERS

Medium, The New York Times, The Washington Post, Bloomberg, Reuters, The Economist and the Financial Times. The Archive.is mirrors work on a wider range of sites, with less reliable results.

FEATURES

• Mirror selector — choose between Freedium Mirror, Freedium, Archive.is, and Archive.is (Alt)
• Auto-fallback — if your selected mirror fails, the app automatically tries the next one in order
• Try Different Mirror button — manually cycle through mirrors from the error screen
• Deep link support — article links from any supported publisher open directly in the app
• Share sheet integration — share an article link to the app from any browser or app
• Reading history — every article you open is saved with its title, domain, and timestamp
• Bookmarks — bookmark articles while reading and access them from the home screen
• Bookmark categories — file bookmarks into categories and filter the list by them
• Home screen feed — recent articles and bookmarks shown at a glance
• Search — live search across all history and bookmark entries
• Export / Import — back up history, bookmarks, categories, reading positions and settings as a JSON file
• Text zoom — adjust article font size from 50% to 200%
• Remember reading position — scroll position saved and restored per article
• Copy as Markdown — copy a whole article to the clipboard as markdown
• Hide site popups — optionally suppress the mirror site's announcement toasts
• Auto-paste — optionally fill the URL box from your clipboard when the app opens

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Open source under the MIT license.
